# Supplementary Archive Manifest

This anonymous-review archive is rooted at `pHRI/saturation` and contains the
materials needed to inspect the paper and regenerate its deterministic study:

- `README.md`: scope, reproduction commands, outputs, and test command;
- `requirements.txt`: Python dependencies;
- `predictive_saturation_paper_v4.md`: manuscript source;
- `paper.css` and `simulation/build_paper_pdf.py`: local PDF build inputs;
- `simulation/saturation_benchmark.py`: models, controllers, scenarios, audits,
  and metrics;
- `simulation/run_all_experiments.py`: complete 111-case experiment driver;
- `simulation/test_saturation_benchmark.py`: 12-test regression suite, including
  a check that the matched reference governor responds to a future scheduled
  actuator-limit drop that its one-step version cannot yet see and a check that
  the seeded held-out plant perturbations remain inside the declared boxes;
- `results/all_experiment_metrics.json`: saved numerical source of record;
- `results/representative_logs.npz`: saved representative trajectories; and
- `results/*.png`: figures generated by the experiment driver and used by the
  manuscript.

The archive's SHA-256 digest is distributed in the adjacent
`predictive_saturation_v4_supplementary.zip.sha256` file.
